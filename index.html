AutoResearch · AI Agent Infrastructure

Beyond using AI as a tool, I build AI systems that do research under verification: a fleet of autonomous research agents whose skills transfer only through independently reproduced results. A new agent must reproduce a published analysis within stated numerical tolerances before it is admitted to production work — capability is earned, not asserted. The output is checkable, not rhetorical: agent-built diagnostic software locked by regression tests, main-text figures sealed as two-minute replay packages, and claims that must survive a falsification pass before they are written down.
