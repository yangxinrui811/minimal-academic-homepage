The Problem: Generation Is Cheap, Verification Is Not

AI-for-Science today is bottlenecked not by intelligence but by trust. A single agent can draft a manuscript, fit a curve, or run a pipeline — but no principal investigator can re-check every number it produces. My answer is institutional, not algorithmic: an organization in which every claim an agent makes about its own skill must be backed by a replayable analysis, reproduced independently, within explicitly stated numerical tolerances.

The Fleet

Ten agents run on five workstations, each with a distinct research mandate. They coordinate through three shared, versioned services — no private channels, everything auditable in git.

How a Skill Travels

The core loop of the fleet — capability becomes a versioned, transferable asset:

1
Work

An agent executes a research task on its own mandate — simulation, analysis, or writing.

2
Submit

Procedures crystallize into skills, submitted with run records: dates, projects, outcomes.

3
Review

The editor-agent deduplicates, merges variants, keeps every contributor's hard-won pitfalls, and versions the result.

4
Install

Any agent fleet-wide can install the skill — and improvements flow back as versioned upgrades.

↺  When the fleet fails, the failure itself is captured as a quantified validation rule — the system writes its own debugging manual. Agents have already caught and patched infrastructure defects this way.

The Portfolio: What the Fleet Has Delivered

Mechanisms are the how — this is the what. Completed campaigns across computational chemistry, each one mandated by a human, executed end-to-end by agents, and closed with a verifiable artifact: an audit script, a replay package, or a regression suite.

Molecular Dynamics & Enhanced Sampling

GROMACS + PLUMED campaigns, from cluster submission to judgment figures

  • Nanoscale-confinement campaign, 90 trajectories. An agent executed the full analysis of a 90-run campaign across a systematic parameter grid — unified time-window alignment, region masking, stratified block statistics — distilled into a five-panel main figure. Two internal audits (26/26 and 51/51 checks) passed before the result was sealed as the fleet's first replay package.
  • Enhanced sampling on public benchmarks. Multi-arm well-tempered metadynamics with convergence races on standard test systems (alanine peptides, chignolin), umbrella-window free energies, and sampling loops redirected by convergence diagnostics — jobs submitted and monitored on the university cluster under strict queue discipline.
  • Trajectory forensics. Published simulation trajectories acquired and re-analyzed locally, auditing published conclusions against their own raw data.

Quantum Chemistry & Reaction Kinetics

Electronic-structure and rate-theory pipelines that run unattended

  • End-to-end kinetics automation. Transition-state search → barrier geometry → tunneling correction → temperature-dependent rate constants, chained across reaction families without per-reaction human touch.
  • Parameter-swept barrier campaigns. At each value of an external control parameter the complete stationary-point network is re-optimized and re-characterized; batches run unattended, with a self-checking watchdog on a 90-minute cadence that reports completion and triages failures.
  • ML potentials with an anti-bootstrap fence. Ab-initio MD → training → path-integral exploration → outlier screening → first-principles re-labeling. Model predictions are never fed back as training labels — the classic self-training trap is fenced off by explicit rule.
  • ML prediction of kinetic isotope effects, from fitting through uncertainty reporting.

Methodology & Theory

Long-running theory programs, versioned like software

  • An 11-iteration theory program on whether enhanced-sampling coordinates miss slow modes — mathematical foundations, sampling protocols, and a regression-tested diagnostics package (CV Check, 18/18) grown from the same line of work.
  • Negative results are archived, not buried. A four-arm benchmark campaign was closed by the principal investigator's verdict after adjudication against ground truth; a six-arm study that disproved the fleet's own efficiency hypothesis lives on as curated documentation. The fleet remembers its failures on purpose.

Machine Learning × Sampling

Where learned models meet enhanced sampling — with audits between

  • Learned slow coordinates, audited. Machine-learned dictionaries of slow modes propose new collective variables; formal audits decide whether they are admitted. The ML proposes, the audit disposes.
  • Diagnostics in the training objective. Convergence and coverage diagnostics made differentiable and embedded directly in the objective of learned collective variables.
  • Closed-loop adaptive sampling. The audit — not a fixed schedule — chooses where the next simulation window opens.

Research Operations

The scientific process around the science — also agent-run

  • Literature intelligence at scale. A curated base of 254 abstracts and 197 full texts; 13 rounds of quantitative deep reading that extracts barriers, tunneling factors, and rate constants from full texts rather than abstracts.
  • Manuscript engineering. A physics manuscript taken through eight structured revision rounds — 11 → 9 pages, 60 references restyled to a single rigorous journal format, four figures normalized, and three silent layout defects caught and fixed.
  • Proposal and document production. Complete drafting cycles for three funding proposals with programmatic document engineering; bilingual CV pipelines; research poster production.

Confidential by design. Unpublished systems and numerical results are never named here — what is shown is the pipeline, the scale, and the checks. Most of the underlying papers are still unpublished; the portfolio describes the craft, not the science.

Design Principles

Verification over generation

The admission currency is a number reproduced independently, within tolerance — never a confident paragraph.

Institutions, not prompts

Editorial review, handover protocols, attribution rules: organizational design is what makes ten agents more than ten chatbots.

Numbers or it didn't happen

Every rule in the knowledge base carries quantitative evidence — an error signature, a threshold, a measured rate.

Human sovereignty

The fleet proposes; the principal investigator disposes. One human reviews decisions, not derivations.

Shipped Artifacts: Evidence a Skeptic Can Poke

Anyone can claim agents do science. These are three things the fleet actually produced — each one checkable, none of them a demo.

Agent-built scientific software

CV Check — a diagnostics package asking whether a simulation's collective variables miss slow modes. Written and iterated by a worker agent: config-driven CLI, tutorial, worked examples, and 18/18 regression tests that pin the implementation to validated reference values.

Sealed replay packages

Main-text figures are sealed as reproduction units: raw inputs, scripts, and an anchor checker that exits non-zero on any miss. A full figure replays from scratch on a plain Linux box in about two minutes — every quantitative anchor must land inside tolerance. Trust, but replay.

Falsification-first review

A family of skills exists to attack results before they are written down: key-variable claims, correlational "discoveries", and artifact-prone analyses must survive a dedicated falsification pass before entering any manuscript, report, or proposal.

Behind them: 33 skills promoted to the verified shared library out of 117 submissions reviewed — every merge keeps its contributor's hard-won pitfalls.

What It Runs

The fleet is not a demo — it produces the science described on the research page: automated transition-state search and tunneling kinetics with AutoTunnel, machine-learning prediction of kinetic isotope effects, machine-learning potentials with path-integral molecular dynamics, and large-scale literature intelligence.
